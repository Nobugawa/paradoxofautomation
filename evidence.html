POA Evidence Tracker

A running record of developments that bear on the Paradox of Automation—what has actually happened, what may be an early signal, what research shows, and what policymakers or companies are preparing for.

The standard: evidence is not all the same. We separate observed events from emerging signals, exposure research and policy scenarios. Each entry says why it matters and, just as importantly, what it does not prove.

Observed developmentA documented event, deployment or statement that has already occurred.
Emerging signalEvidence consistent with a hypothesis, but not enough to establish causation.
Research exposureMeasured exposure or task evidence. Exposure is not the same as job loss.
Policy signalA proposal or preparedness action showing an institution considers a risk plausible enough to plan for.

No evidence entry matches those filters yet. That does not mean there is no evidence; it means nothing in the current POA tracker matches this combination.
